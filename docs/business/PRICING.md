# Pricing Strategy

## Pricing Philosophy

1. **Free tier must deliver real value** — exposure scan + limited aliases. Users see the problem (their data is everywhere) and get a taste of the solution.
2. **Paid tiers unlock depth, not breadth** — free users can do basic protection. Paid users get automation, AI intelligence, and counter-ops.
3. **No feature walls on security** — if a user's data is breached, we alert them even on the free tier. We don't hold safety hostage to upsell.
4. **Family plans are profitable** — families have the highest LTV and lowest churn. Elder protection is a killer feature for the family tier.

## Key Pricing Decisions to Make

1. **Annual discount depth:** Currently 20% for annual. Could go to 30% to reduce churn.
2. **Free tier limits:** 3 email aliases may be too generous or too stingy. A/B test.
3. **Pro vs Individual gap:** Is $5/mo enough to justify Pro? May need clearer feature differentiation.
4. **Enterprise pricing model:** Per-seat vs. flat tier? Need market research.
5. **Student/nonprofit discount:** 50% off? Good for brand, costs little.
