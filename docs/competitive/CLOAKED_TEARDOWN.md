# Competitive Teardown: Phantom vs Cloaked

## Cloaked Overview

Cloaked is an all-in-one privacy platform founded in 2020 by Arjun and Abhijay Bhatnagar. As of March 2026, they've raised $375M in Series B funding, have 350K+ paying customers, and have processed 50M+ scam calls through their Call Guard feature.

## Cloaked's Weaknesses (Our Opportunities)

### 1. Shallow Depth
Cloaked tries to be all-in-one but none of its features compete with specialized tools. Their data removal covers only ~140 of 750+ brokers. Their identity theft protection is just an insurance policy. Their call screening has mixed reviews.

**Phantom's approach:** Go deeper on each vertical. 300+ brokers. AI that learns your patterns. Counter-ops that nobody else offers.

### 2. No Network Effect
Cloaked treats each user as isolated. There's no cross-user intelligence, no collective defense. If 1000 users get the same scam call, Cloaked doesn't learn from the pattern.

**Phantom's approach:** Cross-user threat intelligence network. Anonymized pattern recognition. Pre-protection for emerging threats.

### 3. Clunky UX
App store reviews consistently cite: confusion during setup, error messages, steep learning curve, difficulty understanding features.

**Phantom's approach:** Desktop-first means more screen real estate for clear UX. Extension handles the simple, frequent interactions. Dashboard handles the complex, occasional ones. Progressive disclosure.

### 4. Customer Support Issues
Cancellation difficulties, slow response times, AI chatbot loops. Users can't reach humans.

**Phantom's approach:** Human escalation path guaranteed. In-dashboard chat with real agents during business hours. Clear cancellation flow (one click).

### 5. No Offensive Capability
Cloaked only defends. It blocks calls and removes data, but doesn't fight back, waste scammer time, or contribute to the anti-fraud ecosystem.

**Phantom's approach:** The Scammer Engagement Engine is our most differentiated feature. Entertainment value + community defense + actionable intelligence.

### 6. Mobile-First Limitations
Cloaked launched mobile-first and their app is buggy. Desktop power users who manage dozens of accounts from their browser are underserved.

**Phantom's approach:** Desktop-first captures power users. Extension meets them where exposure happens (in the browser). Mobile comes later, polished.
